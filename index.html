How to Crack the Vault

Your objective is to place digits 1 through 9 into the 3x3 vault grid so every position matches the secret combination.

• Green = Correct position! The tumbler locks permanently.
• Yellow = The digit is correct, but belongs in an adjacent (touching) square.

Tap a tumbler square to select it, then tap a number from the pool below to place or swap numbers instantly. When ready, click Test Combination!
